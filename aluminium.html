Aluminium’s advantages in machining operations

Apart from its superior machinability, aluminium possesses a number of properties that make it ideal for use in machining processes.

High strength-to-weight ratio

Crucial for machined parts used in high-performance applications such as those found in the aerospace and automotive industries.

Corrosion resistance

Aluminium is available in a variety of grades, each of which demonstrates a different level of corrosion resistance. One of the most frequently used grades — 6061 — provides high corrosion resistance during machining processes.

Electrical conductivity

About 37.7 million siemens per meter at ambient temperature, against carbon steel at 7 million and stainless steel at 1.5 million. This enables machined aluminium components to be used as electrical and electronic components.

Surface finishing and anodization potential

Aluminium readily accepts a variety of surface treatment and finishing procedures, including painting, tinting and anodizing.

Recyclable

Aluminium is highly recyclable, allowing manufacturers to repurpose scrap material generated during machining processes.

Precision infrastructure

An outstanding infrastructure and an array of manual and CNC machines produce aluminium machined parts to a very accurate precision.
